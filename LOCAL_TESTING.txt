REYES LAVALUST - LOCAL TESTING

1. Extract the project into Laragon's www folder.
   Example:
   C:\laragon\www\Reyes_Lavalust

2. Start Apache in Laragon.

3. Open Student Home:
   http://localhost/Reyes_Lavalust/student
   or, depending on setup:
   http://localhost/Reyes_Lavalust/public/student

4. IMPORTANT MIDDLEWARE TEST:
   - Open /student/profile directly by typing it in the address bar.
   - It must NOT open the profile.
   - It redirects to /student and shows an "Access Denied" warning.

   The alias /profile is protected the same way.

5. To access the Student Profile:
   - Go to /student.
   - Click the "Open Protected Profile" button.
   - The button grants a one-time access pass.
   - StudentMiddleware verifies the pass before allowing /student/profile.

6. After the profile loads, the one-time pass is already consumed.
   - Typing /student/profile directly again or refreshing the protected route will be blocked.
   - Click "Open Protected Profile" from Home again to access it.

Required laboratory routes remain:
/student
/student/profile
